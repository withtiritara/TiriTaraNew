1. Booking and Payment

  • A non-refundable token amount of ₹2,500 is required to confirm your slot on trips announced more than one month before departure. This amount is counted toward the total trip cost.
  • The remaining balance must be paid in full at least two weeks (14 days) before the departure date, or earlier as communicated by our team.
  • All bookings are considered confirmed only after successful payment and receipt of a written booking confirmation from Tiri Tara.
  • Trip pricing is exclusive of 5% GST, which will be applied at the time of final payment.
  • The cost breakdown shared on our trip details page is indicative and meant for transparency. If you skip a meal or opt out of an activity, that portion of the cost is not refundable.

2. Cancellation and Refund Policy

Tiri Tara keeps groups to a maximum of 11 travellers per trip. Every slot matters for budget, group energy, and the overall experience.

  • Cancellation more than 14 days before departure: 75% refund of the total trip cost paid (the ₹2,500 token is excluded and non-refundable in all cases).
  • Cancellation between 7 to 14 days before departure: 50% refund of the total trip cost paid.
  • Cancellation 3 days or fewer before departure: no refund. No exceptions.
  • The ₹2,500 token amount is non-refundable under all circumstances.

Replacement rule: If you cannot join and you find a suitable replacement traveller yourself, someone we believe fits the group, and inform us before the trip begins, we may at our sole discretion process a full refund as a one-time goodwill exception. This is not guaranteed and is reviewed case by case.

3. Rescheduling

In unforeseen personal or external circumstances, you may request to transfer your booking to another available Tiri Tara trip within 60 days of your originally planned journey date.

  • Rescheduling requests are subject to availability and are reviewed and approved at Tiri Tara's discretion.
  • The rescheduled trip must be taken within 60 days of the original departure date. After this window, the booking will lapse and is no longer eligible for rescheduling or refund.
  • We do not offer automatic rescheduling. All requests must be made in writing.

4. Trip Cancellation by Tiri Tara

  • Tiri Tara is not liable for additional costs incurred by participants, including flights, personal accommodation, travel insurance, or leave taken in anticipation of the trip.
